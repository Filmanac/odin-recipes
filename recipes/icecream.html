Ice Cream Recipe

This is an ice cream recipe, based on the notion you have some in the freezer.

Ingredients

  • Freezer
  • Ice cream (any flavour)
  • Spoon (any size)

Steps

  1. Open your freezer
  2. Grab the ice cream
  3. Close the freezer
  4. Grab your spoon of choice (either from in a drawer, on the draining board, or from the dishwasher)
  5. Open ice cream
  6. Insert spoon
  7. Eat
